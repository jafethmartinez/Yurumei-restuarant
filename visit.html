How long it takes from where you are

Cruise passengers

Yes, you have time.

From Mahogany Bay or the Port of Roatán it is about 45 to 50 minutes each way. Leave in the morning, eat at noon, and you are back at the ship with hours to spare.

Two things to plan for: machuca takes 30 to 45 minutes to cook, and taxi drivers on the east end are not always waiting where you left them. If you want the whole thing handled, our family's tour company can drive you out, wait, and take you back.
