The menu

Everything from the sea depends on what the boats brought in, so some days a dish is on and some days it isn't. Ask — we will tell you what is good today.

Two things worth knowing before you order

Machuca and the stews are made to order. Count on 30 to 45 minutes. Order a beer, look at the collection, watch the boats.

Ask about king crab and lobster. They are seasonal and they depend on the day — when they are in, they are the best thing on this menu.
